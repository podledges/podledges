signal high on active days, commits/week ghosted on the data bus.

## 🃏 the table

Blackjack against the README. Anyone can sit down — the table is shared,
the P&L is eternal, and abandoning a live hand counts as a loss.
The lit buttons are your legal moves; every card comes off a
`SystemRandom`-shuffled deck.
